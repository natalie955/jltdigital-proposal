The Brief

What You Asked For

Five requirements came out of the brief and the discovery call. All five are native GoHighLevel functionality — no Zapier, no Make, no custom development.

Two sales flows, automated

CNC & Signage share one flow with open-text intake and two site-visit loops. Dye Sub runs separately on a structured tick-box intake. Both get timed follow-ups between the salesperson's own touchpoints.

Sequences that stop on reply

The moment a client responds, the automation stands down and hands control back to the sales team. Built on GoHighLevel's native goal-event logic — this is exactly what it exists for.

A Recurring Clients board

A new pipeline mirroring the Sales board — order placed, quoted, paid — carrying the same email and WhatsApp logic, so repeat customers stop being handled from memory.

WhatsApp opt-in / opt-out

A tag-based switch per client, applied consistently across both the new-lead and recurring boards, so nobody is ever messaged on a channel they've asked you not to use.

Abandoned vs Lost, split properly

Six-way routing across both flows — said no outright, or simply went quiet — and which of those happened as a lead, a quote or an invoice. Fall-off finally becomes visible.

All five are native to GoHighLevel — this is a workflow configuration build, not a software development build.

Dependencies

What Moves the Timeline

The hours are fixed, but three things still shape the calendar. All three are visible in week one — which is exactly why the audit comes first.

What the reseller actually granted

The account is a white-labelled GoHighLevel instance bought through a reseller. Depending on the tier that was sold, admin-level settings can be restricted — API access, sub-accounts, billing visibility. We confirm what's exposed before we design around it, not after.

Meta's template approval queue

WhatsApp is already connected and templates are already in use, which removes the biggest unknown. But every new template variant still has to clear Meta approval before it can send outside the 24-hour reply window — 24 to 48 hours per submission, entirely outside our control. The more fall-off scenarios you want covered, the more submissions there are.

How tangled the existing workflows are

The account has been altered before, and nobody currently knows exactly what's running in it. The more legacy automation there is, the more checking each new workflow needs to prove it isn't double-enrolling the same contact into two sequences at once.
