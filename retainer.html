What's Included

Fixed Every Month,
Whatever Else Happens.

Four things come as standard before a single hour is spent on requests. These are the reason the account never drifts back into being a mystery nobody can explain.

A monthly health check

We go in and look: are workflows firing, is anything erroring silently, has a contact been enrolled twice, are the WhatsApp templates still approved. You get a short written note either way — including the months when the answer is simply "all fine".

Priority response

One working day to a human reply, not a ticket number. If something is actively broken and costing leads, it jumps the queue — that is the whole point of holding time in reserve.

Documentation kept current

Every change we make gets written into the handover document the same month. The last person left without explaining anything; the point of this line is that it can never happen again, including if we are the ones who leave.

Your champions stay trained

New staff get shown the system, and existing champions get a refresher whenever something changes. Staff turnover is the usual reason a working setup quietly stops being used a year later.

Four hours to spend

After the health check, the remaining time is yours to direct at whatever matters most that month — see the next section for what that actually buys.

No lock-in, no minimum term beyond the first month, two months' notice to stop.

What It Isn't

Where the Retainer Stops

Said plainly up front, so nobody discovers a boundary at the point they need it most.

It is not a build project

Four hours a month improves a system steadily; it will not construct a large one from scratch. A full multi-flow sales automation build runs to dozens of hours and belongs in its own quoted project. The retainer is for keeping things working, extending them, and adding a piece at a time.

It does not cover platform costs

GoHighLevel and reseller fees, WhatsApp's roughly $10 per sub-account each month, and Meta's per-message charges outside the 24-hour window all stay on the client's own account at the vendor's price. We never resell or mark them up.

It is not marketing or accounts

Google and Meta campaign work stays with you, and Xero stays manual as the client asked. The retainer covers automation inside GoHighLevel — workflows, pipelines, templates, tags and training.

When a request is too big. Anything beyond roughly ten banked hours stops being retainer work and gets quoted as a small project instead — agreed in writing before it starts, at the same R500 rate. That keeps the monthly cost genuinely fixed rather than a figure that creeps upward every time something substantial is asked for.
Terms

Three Months, Then It's Rolling.

There is a short initial term, and it is worth explaining why rather than burying it.

The first month is largely us learning the account — what is running, what is tagged, what the previous developer left behind. That work benefits you every month afterwards, but it is front-loaded, and a one-month retainer would mean paying for the learning and leaving before the value. Three months is the shortest honest term: long enough to get past the onboarding and see two or three real pieces of work land. After that it runs month to month on one month’s notice, indefinitely, with no renewal to sign and nothing that auto-escalates.
